Common questions

How does ticket verification work?

When you list a ticket, our AI scans the screenshot to confirm it shows a valid QR code and event date. Tickets that don't pass verification are automatically rejected and the image is deleted.

When do I get paid as a seller?

Your payout is released after the buyer successfully enters the event. Funds are transferred to your account within 1-3 business days.

What if my ticket is rejected by the AI scan?

Make sure your screenshot clearly shows a QR code and the event date. Screenshots from RA, Fever, Eventbrite, and Dice work best. If you believe your ticket was incorrectly rejected, contact us at hello@cluboapp.org.

How do I get a refund?

If the seller's ticket doesn't work at the door, you're covered. Open a dispute in the app within 2 hours of the event and we'll review it and issue a refund if warranted.

Why do I need a .edu email?

Clubo is exclusively for verified university students. Your .edu Google account is how we confirm you're a student — no personal Gmail or other email addresses are accepted.

How do I opt out of SMS messages?

Reply STOP to any SMS from Clubo to opt out. You can also contact us at hello@cluboapp.org. Note that opting out of SMS will disable login via OTP.
